## Findings against overkillhill.com/projects/glee-fully-chai-chasers/

### Wrong, and needs correcting

**O1. UniGlee rarity.** Live page: *"a rare event occurring approximately once per 4,200 spins"* and *"shows itself often but is only truly caught."*

The engine rolls three independent per-reel captures at 1/2,500, 1/4,000, and 1/7,500. Combined rate is **~1 in 1,277**, measured at 1 in 1,370 by the oracle and 1 in 1,479 across the sim fleet. There is **no "tease" or decorative sighting mechanic anywhere in the codebase** (grep for `tease`, `sighting`: zero hits). `placeUniGleeTrigger` explicitly makes the trigger line-valid *"so the event cannot be a decorative, non-paying-looking scatter."* The live copy describes the opposite of what the code does.

**O2. The 103% RTP claim.** Live page: *"permitted to elevate overall measured RTP to roughly 103% when landed."*

Measured full-game RTP with UniGlee fully played out is **95.66%**. Highest single seed out of seven was 100.37%. Nothing in the current engine produces 103%. This claim is the most exposed one on the page because it is the one a skeptical reader would try to reproduce.

**O3. Decision count.** Live page: *"Thirty-four documented decisions."*

`docs/DECISION-LOG.md` contains **33 settled rows spanning S1 through S32**, with two rows both labeled S30. There is no S33 and no S34. The page's own acceptance criterion was *"a reader who fact-checks the page against the game repo's DECISION-LOG.md finds zero stale claims."* That criterion currently fails.

**Root cause for O1–O3:** `content/PRD-OVERKILLHILL-PROJECT-PAGE-PATCH-2.md` was written and shipped describing decisions S33/S34 and an engine change that never landed in this repository. The page was patched; the code was not. Either the decisions were made and never logged, or the patch was written against a proposal that got dropped. Worth resolving in the decision log before the page is patched again.

### Correct, leave alone

- *"The final build passed 149 tests"* is scoped explicitly as a ship-week historical claim in Patch 2 §0. It stays.
- *"nine calendar days"*, the July 9–10 research window, and the 14% RTP failure story are all accurate history.
- Stack, hosting, license, and the tool-assignment table are accurate.
- The five governance artifacts and five principles read true against the repo.

### Missing

The page is a case study that stops at ship week. Everything since 2026-07-16 is invisible: five shipped bonus chapters, the XP economy, a threat model (`docs/threat-model.md`), a supply-chain posture (pnpm `minimumReleaseAge: 1440`, two CVE overrides), an ADR practice (`docs/adr/`), a promoted skill (`skills/okhp3-skill-promotion/`), a showcase video, and a test suite that grew from 149 to 170. For a page whose whole argument is *governance produces durable results*, a month of post-gift maintenance is the strongest available evidence and it is not on the page.
