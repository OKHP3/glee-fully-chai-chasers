Special symbols

Treat — Chicken

Sends a chicken comet to the treat jar. Feed the cats when they visit!

Treat — Salmon

Fills the jar with salmon stars. A cat favourite, especially Phoebe's.

Treat — Bougie

Rare. Joey appears immediately with an impressed quip. Worth it.

Firefly Wild

Substitutes for all paying symbols. Each Wild collected fills the Firefly Cascade meter by 1.

Phoebe Wild

Wild on reels 3–5 only. Expands to fill the reel and triggers a re-spin with Phoebe held.

Joey Wild

Wild on reels 1–3 only. Doubles all wins on the re-spin when held.

All paylines pay left to right. Longest win per line only. Treat symbols are non-paying.
