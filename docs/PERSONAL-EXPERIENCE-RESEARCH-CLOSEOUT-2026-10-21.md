# Personal experience research closeout

**Research completed:** September 6, 2026

**Closeout recorded:** October 21, 2026

**Status:** research complete; design recommendations remain proposals

**Decision owner:** Jamie

## Outcome

The investigation considered how the existing cascading-reels birthday game
could become a more personal experience for Glee. It compared reference-game
patterns, inspected the then-current implementation, reviewed player-experience
and accessibility guidance, and evaluated available skills.

The recommendation is to preserve the existing cascade foundation and improve
character behavior, readable cause and effect, chapter transitions, original
audio, and opportunities to revisit personal moments. Whether Glee prefers a
proposed change remains untested. No implementation, probability change,
framework replacement, or new release was part of the research deliverable.

## Recommendations for a later assignment

- Make Joey and Phoebe's established personalities visible in the actions that
  produce existing effects. Each intervention should make its cause, effect,
  and outcome understandable without obscuring the board.
- Compare an existing cascade sequence and Laundry Helper presentation with a
  bounded prototype using identical deterministic outcomes in a development
  harness. Keep forced outcomes out of ordinary play.
- Scope active-session persistence and pacing controls before expanding the
  presentation of long celebrations. Reload and resume must preserve reward
  accounting; skipping an interactive section requires an explicit resolution
  policy rather than an assumption of perfect play.
- Consider an unlocked-scene collection and replay flow, original character
  motifs, and a short optional ending that feels warm regardless of payout.
- Evaluate tapping, memory challenges, pacing, recognition, and replay interest
  with Glee's own feedback. Collect observations privately and manually under
  the existing analytics policy.

These recommendations are consistent with the approved Chai Chase direction.
They do not create new release commitments or assign deferred implementation
work to this research thread.

## Supporting research and skills

The research used the [Player Experience of Needs Satisfaction overview](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/player-experience-of-needs-satisfaction-pens/)
from the Center for Self-Determination Theory as a source of design hypotheses,
alongside [Tyack and Mekler's 2024 critique of theory use in games research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.12639).
The [Game Accessibility Guidelines](https://gameaccessibilityguidelines.com/full-list/)
informed input and pacing options. These sources were consulted during the
September investigation; they are not evidence of Glee's individual preferences.

Deep Research, find-skills, skill discovery, and evidence classification supported
the investigation. Existing frontend design and testing skills were suitable
for a future bounded prototype. No new skill installation or FoundRy child
repository was necessary. Availability should be checked again when that work
is assigned.
